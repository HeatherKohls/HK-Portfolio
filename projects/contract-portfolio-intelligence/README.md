# Contract Portfolio Intelligence & Renewal Management

**KPI Design | Renewal Governance | Revenue Analytics | Power BI | Agiloft | Portfolio Optimization**

## Overview

Knowing that a contract was approaching renewal was only part of the decision. The larger question was whether the contract was performing well enough to justify the time, resources and commercial effort required to renew and support it.

I helped build a contract portfolio intelligence process that connected renewal timing, original Sales estimates and actual contract sales so Program Managers and leaders could see portfolio health, focus limited resources and use renewal events to address risk, pricing, revenue and margin opportunities.

## Business challenge

Sales entered an estimated contract value during the bid/RFP process, while actual sales were captured through the sales engine when orders were coded to the contract. Contract and renewal information was tracked in Agiloft, but the underlying systems did not yet communicate cleanly enough to support reliable portfolio reporting.

Leadership needed a practical way to see 30/60/90-day renewal demand, compare expected value with actual performance, and understand portfolio health across **State, Local, Commercial and Federal** contracts.

The team could not wait for a perfect future-state integration, so the operating process and quality controls had to work while APIs and automation were still being developed.

## Operating model

**Capture → Validate → Analyze → Act → Learn**

1. **Capture** — Sales estimate, contract and renewal data, and actual sales.
2. **Validate** — QC and reporting controls bridge technology gaps.
3. **Analyze** — Power BI provides portfolio-level KPIs and visibility.
4. **Act** — Program Managers review renewals, document actions, identify risks and surface opportunities.
5. **Learn** — Actual contract performance informs future pursuit and RFP assumptions.

## KPIs and decisions

- **30 / 60 / 90-day renewal pipeline** — What requires action now and what is approaching.
- **Renewed vs. pending** — Where renewal activity is progressing and where follow-up is needed.
- **Initial estimate vs. actual sales** — Whether original opportunity assumptions translated into real contract performance.
- **State / Local / Commercial / Federal** — Where portfolio value, performance, renewal exposure and opportunity are concentrated by market.
- **Portfolio by Program Manager** — Where workload, risk and opportunity are concentrated by owner.
- **Top vs. bottom contracts** — Where limited resources should be focused.
- **Renewal risk + pricing / margin opportunity** — Whether renewal should be used to remediate risk or improve contract economics.

## From dashboard to management process

The dashboard was not intended to be passive reporting. Program Managers reviewed their portfolios, documented actions against outstanding renewals, identified risks to renew, and surfaced opportunities to improve pricing, revenue and margin.

The process helped the team evaluate renewal viability using actual contract performance rather than contract value alone, prioritize limited resources, and use the gap between original opportunity estimates and actual sales as evidence when evaluating future RFPs and pursuit assumptions.

> **RFP / Sales Estimate → Contract Award → Actual Sales → Portfolio KPI → 30/60/90 Renewal Review → Risk & Opportunity Action → Renewal / Pricing Decision → Actual Performance → Better Future RFP Intelligence**

## Technology and controls

**Agiloft** supported contract and renewal tracking. **Power BI** provided portfolio-level dashboarding. Actual sales were captured through the sales engine when orders were coded to the contract.

Because the systems did not yet fully communicate, we developed interim reporting and QC controls while APIs were being built to automate the data flow. The key transformation was not the dashboard alone; it was establishing the definitions, controls, review cadence and accountability required to make the information useful before the technology environment was fully integrated.
