# Contract Launch & Lifecycle Governance Transformation

**Contract Operations | SOP & Governance | Post-Award Management | RACI | Smartsheet | AI Enablement**

## Overview

Launching a contract was much more than getting a signature. Once an award came in, the work had to become something Sales, Finance, Services, Program Management and the customer could actually use.

The challenge was that the work crossed multiple teams and systems that did not fully talk to each other. Program Management was not always involved early enough in negotiations even though the team ultimately owned many of the SLAs, reporting requirements, fees and other obligations once the contract was live.

I designed an end-to-end process for taking an agreement from award notification through negotiation, signature, launch, ongoing contract management and eventually closeout.

## What the case demonstrates

- End-to-end contract launch and lifecycle governance
- Cross-functional RACI and ownership
- Signature and handoff controls
- SOP/playbook and launch-checklist design
- Risk-based contract prioritization
- Smartsheet workflow visibility
- KPI and intake-date controls
- Training, adoption and change management
- After-action reviews and continuous improvement
- Standardization as a foundation for automation and AI

## Transformation timeline

**0–3 months — Map & improve:** Mapped the award-to-launch process while the team continued supporting active contracts, renewals and RFPs. Smaller improvements were made as gaps became clear rather than waiting for a perfect future state.

**By 6 months — Full lifecycle & buy-in:** The full lifecycle was mapped with stakeholder buy-in and key contract controls working, providing much better visibility into where an agreement was and what still needed to happen.

**Within 12 months — Adoption & scale:** The process had strong adoption across the Program Management team and the broader matrixed organization through training, office hours and one-on-one support.

**Next evolution — Standardize & automate:** Side trackers were standardized and began moving into a common Smartsheet workflow, creating a cleaner foundation for automation and AI.

## Design principle

> **Standardize the controls, not the complexity.**

Every agreement needs the basics to launch correctly, but the amount of review, training and governance should scale with financial impact, customer impact, contractual risk and complexity.

## End-to-end launch flow

Award notification → negotiations & business review → signature routing → fully executed handoff → systems/compliance setup → finance & billing setup → Sales/Services enablement → ongoing governance → closeout

## Results

The work created stronger visibility into what was coming, who owned it, what still needed to happen and where the risk was. It also created a repeatable operating model that could be trained, measured, improved and eventually automated.

## From standardization to automation

Once the lifecycle, templates and side trackers were more consistent, the process was in a much better position to automate. The standardized process maps, training, templates and field requirements now provide structured inputs for IT and AI-enabled work intended to reduce repetitive data entry and improve workflow and follow-up management.
