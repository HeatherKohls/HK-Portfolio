# Contract Governance & Operations

This section demonstrates how I approach contract governance across both sides of signature: making disciplined, risk-informed decisions before an agreement is executed and translating the executed agreement into controlled operational work after signature.

## Case Study 2A — Contract Launch & Lifecycle Governance Transformation

An end-to-end operating model for moving agreements from award notification through negotiation, signature, operational launch, ongoing governance and closeout.

**What it demonstrates**
- Contract launch and lifecycle governance
- SOP/playbook and checklist design
- Cross-functional RACI and handoff controls
- Risk-based prioritization
- Change management and adoption
- Workflow visibility and standardization
- Foundation for automation and AI

[View Case Study 2A](contract-launch-governance/README.md)

## Case Study 2B — RFP-to-Signature Governance & Decision Process

A structured, Shipley-informed stage-gate framework for bringing Sales, Finance, Legal, Compliance and Program Management into RFP and contract decisions at the appropriate points.

**What it demonstrates**
- Pursue / do-not-pursue governance
- Risk-informed approval gates
- Cross-functional accountability
- Financial, legal, compliance and delivery review
- Contract-value-driven involvement
- Negotiation and signature controls
- Pre-award to post-award handoff
